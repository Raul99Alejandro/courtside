# Visual direction (gate 2, approved by the owner 2026-10-05)

**Choice:** a mix. Base **A · Floodlight**, with **C's scarf colours** as each viewer's identity and **B's marker annotations** on the pitch.

## Tokens

- Colours (from A): night `#0E1A14`, pitch `#1E5631`, stripe `#24683B`, chalk `#F2F5EE`, floodlight `#FFD36B`, board `#13241B`.
- Viewer scarf colours (from C), assigned in profile order and kept for life: `#F2C230` yellow, `#7FB8E6` sky, `#FF6B57` coral, then `#F4F6FA` chalk-white; more profiles reuse the list with a stripe pattern.
- Annotation red (from B): `#FF6B6B` (the board's `#D7263D` lifted for contrast on the dark pitch); Kalam for annotations only.
- Type: Barlow Condensed (titles, minute, score), Atkinson Hyperlegible (body), Kalam (pitch annotations only).

## Do

- Each card row starts with the scarf stripe of the viewers on it (two viewers on one row → two stripes side by side).
- "Who's watching?" profile tiles show the viewer's scarf; the phone header shows the viewer's scarf too.
- On the pitch: the actor's name in Kalam with a marker arrow to the actor; for goals and offsides, one short annotation (e.g. "offside line") when the facts support it.
- Keep the floodlight haze subtle behind text; contrast ≥ 4.5:1 on every text.
- 10-foot rules: body ≥ 28 px, titles ≥ 48 px, 5 % safe area, visible focus on every focusable element.

## Don't

- No sticky notes, no whiteboard background, no navy terrace background (those belonged to B and C).
- No more than one annotation colour on the pitch.
- No numbered markers unless the content is a real sequence (fix the phone pre-permission screen).
- No overlap between toasts and other text (fix the level-up toast over the timeline caption).

Owner comments after this point become new rules in this file.
